About SearchLens

SearchLens is a free online web auditing application designed to analyze web pages for search engine optimization (SEO), generative engine optimization (GEO), and answer engine optimization (AEO). Our mission is to provide webmasters and developers with real-time, actionable insights to improve search visibility and AI crawler readability.

Privacy Policy & Terms

SearchLens respects your privacy. We do not store, log, or sell any website source code or URLs entered into our scanner. All client-side audits are processed dynamically in real time. We use standard third-party services like Google AdSense to display non-intrusive advertisements which may use cookies to serve personalized ads.
