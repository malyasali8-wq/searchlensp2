When SEOGlint is a good fit

  • You want a quick, free SEO audit of a single page with clear fixes.
  • You care about AI search: GEO, AEO, llms.txt and AI crawler access.
  • You need to create or fix a robots.txt, sitemap, schema or meta tags. See all free SEO tools.

When you need something else

  • Keyword research, rank tracking and competitor data: use a paid suite.
  • Backlink analysis: use a dedicated backlink tool.
  • Crawling thousands of pages: use a site crawler.

How to choose an SEO tool

Run the same page through two or three tools. Compare what each finds, how clear the fixes are and what is locked behind a plan. Start with the free checks, and pay only for the data you cannot get elsewhere.

Free SEO checker features to look for

  • A clear score with a ranked list of fixes.
  • On-page checks: title, description, headings, links and images.
  • Technical checks: canonical, robots, sitemap, HTTPS and speed signals.
  • Structured data and AI search checks.
  • No sign-up wall before you see the results.

SEO audit by tool vs by hand

A manual SEO audit gives the deepest insight but takes hours. A tool finds the common problems in seconds. A good approach is to run a free audit first, then spend your manual time on the issues that matter. Our 25-point checklist shows what to review by hand.

Frequently asked questions

Is SEOGlint really free?

Yes. All SEOGlint tools are free with no sign-up, and most run in your browser.

What does SEOGlint check that a classic SEO checker does not?

Besides on-page and technical SEO, it checks AI search readiness (GEO and AEO), search experience (SXO), llms.txt and which AI crawlers your robots.txt allows.

What does SEOGlint not do?

It does not provide backlink data, keyword volumes, rank tracking or full-site crawls of thousands of pages. Paid suites and desktop crawlers cover those jobs.

Should I use SEOGlint instead of a paid SEO suite?

Use it for quick page audits, fixes and AI search readiness at no cost. If you need keyword research, backlinks or rank tracking at scale, a paid suite is a good addition.

How do I compare SEO tools fairly?

Run the same page through each tool and compare what it finds, how clear the fixes are and what it costs. Check which checks are free and which are locked.

Can I use SEOGlint together with other SEO tools?

Yes. Many people use SEOGlint for fast page audits and fixes, and a paid suite for keyword and backlink data.
