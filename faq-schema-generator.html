What is FAQ schema?


FAQ schema (FAQPage structured data) is JSON-LD markup that tells Google a page contains a list of questions and answers. When added correctly, it helps search engines understand your Q&A and can make your listing richer — and it maps perfectly to how AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) extract and cite direct answers. It's one of the highest-leverage schema types for both SEO and AI search.


Why use FAQ schema?


    

  • AI citations (AEO/GEO) — clean question/answer pairs are exactly what AI engines lift into answers.
