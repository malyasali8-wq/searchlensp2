About SearchLens

The free SEO, GEO & AEO audit tool.

SearchLens is a free, browser-based auditing tool that scans any web page for three things at once: classic search engine optimization (SEO), technical health, and readiness for AI-powered search — what the industry now calls GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization).

You paste a page's HTML or enter a live URL, and in a single scan SearchLens returns a 0–100 score broken down by On-Page SEO, Technical & Schema, and AI-search readiness — each with plain-English evidence and a ready-to-paste fix.

Why we built it

Search is changing fast. A growing share of searches now end inside an AI answer — Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude — instead of a traditional blue link. Most SEO tools were built for the old model and say nothing about whether AI engines can actually read and cite your page. SearchLens was built to close that gap: to check the classic signals and the new AI-search signals in one place, for free.

Who it's for

  • Website owners & bloggers who want a quick, honest read on where a page stands.
  • Developers shipping new pages who need a pre-launch checklist.
  • SEO & marketing professionals who want an AI-readiness layer their usual tools don't cover.

How it stays free

The audit runs in your browser, so there's no heavy server cost. SearchLens is supported by non-intrusive advertising. We don't charge, and we don't require sign-up. See our Privacy Policy for exactly how data is handled.

Who's behind it

SearchLens is built and maintained by an independent SEO and web-development practitioner focused on the shift from classic search to AI search. Questions or feedback are genuinely welcome — reach us on the Contact page.
