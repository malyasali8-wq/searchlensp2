Free SEO tools for beginners and pros

A good free SEO tool does one job clearly. Use the audit to see the big picture, then open the tool that fixes each issue. Online SEO tools like these cover the on-page and technical basics: titles and descriptions, crawling rules, sitemaps, structured data and page speed signals.

What each group of free website SEO tools does

  • Audit: the free SEO audit scans a page for SEO, GEO, AEO and SXO issues and gives a score.
  • Crawling: the robots.txt generator and sitemap generator tell search engines what to crawl and where your pages are.
  • Structured data: the schema generator, FAQ schema generator and schema validator help search engines and AI understand your content.
  • Snippets and sharing: the meta tag generator and OG checker control how your page looks in results and on social.
  • AI search: the llms.txt generator and AI visibility checker prepare you for ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

How to use online SEO tools together

Online SEO tools work best as a short routine. Run the audit first, fix the biggest problems, then use the matching generator for each fix. Re-run the audit to confirm the score went up.

  1. Audit a page to find what is broken.
  2. Fix crawling with the robots.txt and sitemap tools so search engines can find your pages.
  3. Write clear titles and descriptions with the meta tag tool.
  4. Add and check structured data.
  5. Prepare for AI search with llms.txt and the AI visibility check.

SEO tools for beginners: where to start

If you are new, you do not need ten tools on day one. Start with the free SEO audit, fix the red items, and learn one tool at a time. Our website audit checklist explains each check in plain language, and the GEO guide covers AI search.

What makes a good free website SEO tool?

  • Clear fixes: it tells you what to change, not just what is wrong.
  • No lock-in: you can use it without an account or a trial.
  • Privacy: ideally it runs in your browser so your content stays with you.
  • Current checks: it covers modern needs such as structured data and AI crawlers.
