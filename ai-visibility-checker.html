What is an AI visibility checker?

An AI visibility checker tells you whether AI search engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews, Claude and others — are actually allowed to crawl your website. These engines send their own bots, and if your robots.txt blocks them, your content can never be read, used or cited in AI answers. This tool reads your robots rules and reports, bot by bot, who can see you.

Why AI visibility matters

Search is shifting from blue links to AI answers. A growing share of queries are answered directly by AI that reads sources and cites a few of them. Being one of those cited sources is a fast-growing traffic channel — but only if the AI crawler can reach your pages in the first place. Many sites accidentally block these bots, or block them by default via a restrictive robots.txt, and quietly disappear from AI results.

The AI crawlers this tool checks

  • GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User — OpenAI (training, search, live fetch)
  • ClaudeBot — Anthropic
  • PerplexityBot — Perplexity
  • Google-Extended — controls Google's AI (Gemini / AI Overviews) use, separate from Search
  • CCBot — Common Crawl, used by many AI datasets
  • Bytespider — ByteDance / TikTok

How to fix blocked AI crawlers

If the check shows bots are blocked and you want AI visibility (most sites do), update your robots.txt to allow them. Our robots.txt generator builds the correct rules with one toggle per bot. Then add an llms.txt file to point AI models at your best content, and write answer-first content so you are easy to cite.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if AI can see my website?

Paste your robots.txt into this tool. It checks each major AI crawler (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and more) against your rules and tells you which are allowed or blocked.

Should AI crawlers be allowed?

For most sites, yes — being crawlable is what lets ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews cite you, which is a growing traffic source. Block them only if you specifically don't want your content used by AI.

What is Google-Extended?

A robots.txt token that controls whether Google can use your content for its AI products (Gemini, AI Overviews). Blocking it doesn't affect your normal Google Search ranking.

Is this AI visibility checker free?

Yes — it runs entirely in your browser, needs no sign-up, and nothing you paste is uploaded or stored.
